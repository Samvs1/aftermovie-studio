# Oman, inshallah: treatment (direction C, "Souq pop"), as built in preview v1

**Song:** OneRepublic, "I Lived", album version, played whole from its first note (no joins), then silence for the
thank-you. 120 BPM: bar n starts at 0:00.1 + 2n s (bars counted from 0). Total length 4:11.

## Concept
Two hungry Belgians booked a surprisingly cheap flight, landed in the last ten days of Ramadan, and were fed, guided,
hosted and gifted their way around Oman by strangers. The film is that trip as loud Gulf pop art (after Hassan Hajjaj):
every photo is a print in a tile border on colour fields and tent stripes, facts arrive as price tags, the hooks slam onto
colour blocks, Arabic sits beside English, and die-cut stickers keep score. The Suntop juice box, contraband in
daylight, is the running gag. Trip order throughout, with the lyric lines landing on the trip's own moments.

## Signature moments
1. **The reveal (0:08–0:16).** "Hope when you take that jump" brings CHEAP FLIGHT: BOOKED, then WHY SO CHEAP?. The
   downbeat answers with a slab: RAMADAN | رمضان, and RESTAURANTS: CLOSED UNTIL SUNSET.
2. **Three twins.** SAME WALL (the corniche, 0:19), SAME CAR (the sedan, 0:52), then the chorus payoff SAME ROCK (1:20).
   Each time Sam is followed by Tom in the same pose and place, with the tag in the same spot.
3. **"I, I did it all" (1:08–1:24).** The chorus builds instead of holding. Sam arms wide, then the goats in the tree and
   your two shadows on the canyon, then Tom lands on the same rock while the backdrop flips from saffron to red.
4. **The souq stall (1:24–1:32).** One print per beat on two shelves, in trip order: the night you lay on the road, the
   5 BURGERS + 2 SMOOTHIES night, the desert tour, the sand throws, the jumps. Then I SAW / SO / MANY / PLACES lands word
   by word on a red band, each word on its sung syllable.
5. **Sunset to iftar (1:48–1:56).** A saffron sun disc sinks behind the desert glow, one notch per beat, gone on "down"
   (with إفطار, iftar). On "raise your cup" the hosts' selfie is framed by Suntop boxes popping on in sixteenths: Hajjaj's
   product frame, with AFTER SUNSET: FOOD IS LEGAL.
6. **"I'll say / I!" (2:06 and 2:14).** Sam's cliff jump, then Tom's, mirrored left and right. Each splash hits a chorus
   downbeat as the backdrop flips colour.
7. **"With every broken bone" (2:31).** Sam's barefoot hop over the hot rocks. BROKEN BONE gets marked down with a red
   sale stripe on "broken", and BURNED FEET slaps on.
8. **The Ramadan bookend (2:48).** Bar 6's layout returns (red field, green frame) with RAMADAN / IS OVER. The real
   Suntop on the Eid lawn gets circled: SUNTOP IN PUBLIC.
9. **"I owned every second" (3:20).** The whole trip, day 2 to day 14, one tile per 8th, in order. Then I SAW SO MANY
   PLACES fills the screen as a window onto the dunes and the camera flies through it.
10. **The ending.** Istanbul's sunset on the ring-out, then silence: شكرا / THANK YOU and your approved list of names,
    word for word.

## Device budget
- Single framed print: about 35% of shots.
- Two-up prints (Sam then Tom): about 20%.
- Framed clips: 10 clips, most of them vertical, shown whole in tall frames.
- Full bleed in a tile border: 4 shots.
- Bursts, each a different build: the stall (chorus 1), the dropping prints (chorus 2), the trip wall plus the knockout
  (final chorus).
- Tags: about half the shots, at most two per shot; long facts break onto two lines.
- Stickers: the JBL speaker, the Suntop (5 moments, including the product frame), the boots, ✈️.
- New in this film: Hajjaj tile frames, Arabic slabs, price-tag facts, photo cut-out stickers, the sinking sun, the
  product frame, the colour flips on the chorus downbeats.
