# Oman, inshallah: treatment (direction C, "Souq pop")

**Song:** OneRepublic, "I Lived", album version (3:54). It plays whole from its first note (no joins), then silence for
the thank-you. 120 BPM, so bar n starts at 0:00.1 + 2n s (bars are counted from 0). Total length ≈ 4:10.

## Concept
Two hungry Belgians booked the cheapest flight they could find, landed in the last ten days of Ramadan, and were fed,
driven, guided and gifted their way around Oman by strangers. The film tells that trip like a loud piece of Gulf pop art
(after Hassan Hajjaj): every photo is a print in a tile border, laid on colour fields and tent stripes; facts arrive as
price tags; the song's hooks slam onto colour blocks; Arabic sits beside English; die-cut stickers keep score. The
Suntop juice box, contraband in daylight, is the running gag. Verses are calm (one framed photo per bar). Choruses build
the frame up beat by beat until it bursts, then cut to one quiet photo. Everything runs in trip order, and the lyric
lines out "Hope when…" happen to line up with the trip's own moments.

## Signature moments
1. **The reveal (bar 6, 0:12).** "Hope when you take that jump" lands on the tag CHEAPEST FLIGHT TO MUSCAT: BOOKED, and
   "you don't fear the fall" on WHY SO CHEAP?. The downbeat answers with a slab: RAMADAN | رمضان, and RESTAURANTS: CLOSED
   UNTIL SUNSET.
2. **"I, I did it all" (bars 34–41, 1:08).** Sam slams in, arms wide on the canyon edge, on the first held "I". On the
   second "I", Tom lands beside him: same rock, same red-white-yellow trail mark, same pose.
3. **The souq wall (bars 41.75–45, 1:24).** "I owned every second…": 18 tiles of the desert day stack up like a
   market stall, one per 8th note, until the screen is full. A red band slams I SAW SO MANY PLACES. On "I swear I lived"
   everything drops away to one quiet photo of the two of you on the dune ridge.
4. **"And when that sun goes down / hope you raise your cup" (bars 54–56, 1:48).** A saffron sun disc sinks behind the
   real desert horizon on the four beats of the bar. إفطار (iftar) and AFTER SUNSET: FOOD IS LEGAL appear. On "cup" a
   Suntop sticker raises a toast over your tea in the Bedouin tent.
5. **"Until my moment comes / I'll say / I!" (bars 62–64, 2:04).** Tom stands on the rock edge, takes off on "I'll say",
   and the splash hits the chorus downbeat with I, I DID IT ALL.
6. **"With every broken bone" (bar 76, 2:32).** The barefoot hop across the burning wadi rocks. A tag reads BROKEN BONE, a
   red line strikes it out on the downbeat, and BURNED FEET slaps on.
7. **The ending.** The trip's last sunset, over Istanbul, falls on the song's final hit, followed by silence and شكرا /
   THANK YOU to every stranger who helped.

## Running gags
- **The Suntop juice box** (cut out of your dashboard photo) is daytime contraband. It slaps on whenever you sneak food
  (the goat, the roadside pitstop) and raises a toast at sunset.
- **Ramadan:** set up as WHY SO CHEAP? → RAMADAN, run through the goat witness, DYING / RECOVERING and the pitstop, then
  paid off on Eid: RAMADAN IS OVER: LUNCH IN PUBLIC.
- **Strangers:** every helper gets a tag where it happened (Abdoul, the hosts, Mohammed, the man who paid, the Quran,
  the coffee), and they all return in the thank-you.
- **Feet:** SHOES: OFF → BURNED FEET → the fish only wanted Sam's feet.

## Device budget (share of shots)
- Single framed print: ~30%.
- Two-up prints: ~15%.
- Full-bleed with a tile screen-border: ~12%.
- Framed clips (most of them vertical): ~13%.
- Bursts: 3 (the wall, the stack, the collage), each a different build.
- Slabs: hooks and labels.
- Tags: facts on about 40% of shots, at most two per shot.
- Stickers: about 8 moments (Suntop, boots, JBL).
- The sun disc: twice, as bookends.
- New for your films: the tile-framed Hajjaj prints, the Arabic slabs, the price-tag facts, the photo cut-out stickers
  and the sinking sun.
