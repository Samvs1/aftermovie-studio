## Concept
The trip as the sketchbook you kept on the road. Every stop starts as a **pencil drawing** on a cream dot-grid page. On the beat, the photo's **real colour floods in like watercolour**, blooming from the middle out.

One **red ink line**, your real GPS route, runs from page to page from Tbilisi and back. Red ink is the book's second voice: it carries the route and the punchlines. Everything else is pencil.

**The colour follows the song.**
- The quiet voice-and-guitar opening (Tbilisi) is almost all graphite, with only small touches of colour: the sun, Sameba's gold at night.
- When the kick drum and banjo come in (0:58), colour floods the page for the first time, just as the road trip starts. From then on the pages are painted.

No taped prints, no kraft, no polaroids, no speech bubbles, no car emoji: the first film used those.

## Signature moments
1. **Colour arrives with the band (0:58).** The rental-rules page faces a pencil drawing of the flag over the Gudauri valley. On the kick drum's first downbeat it blooms into full colour: the first full-colour painting in the film.
2. **"And see the world hanging upside down" (2:06).** The Prometheus cave page is drawn upside down: the stalactites point up and the date is upside down. On "upside… down" the whole book turns 180°. Now the cave hangs the right way and the writing reads. Two minutes later, Batumi's upside-down house gets the callback "not the book this time."
3. **The brass climax (2:37–2:59).** The book riffles, one page per bar. Each painting blooms on its downbeat, in order: Tskaltubo's sanatorium, "Yes, no entry.", Batumi, the moon through the Abastumani telescope, Vardzia.
4. **The last chorus (2:59–3:19).** A double page:
   - The left page writes a checklist on the sung phrases: *survived: ✓ the roads ✓ sketchy hotels ✓ taxi drivers ✓ khinkali, every day*.
   - The right page paints the last days, one per bar: Vardzia, Borjomi, "Big chill", Sword mountain.
   - On "I'll know my name as it's called again", you two sign the page.
5. **The last chord (3:19–3:26).** The camera pulls back to the whole loop in red ink, with a small painting at every stop. In the silence the pencil writes **madloba!**, then **Thank you Georgia**, then "— Tom + Sam".

## Look, type, motion
- **Look:**
  - Pages: cream paper with a dot grid.
  - Photos: the new `sketch` look, as graphite drawings that bloom into colour; when finished they keep soft pencil edges.
  - Night pages: white pencil on black paper.
  - `golden` grade, very light grain, no weave.
- **Type:** pencil handwriting (Caveat, graphite) for dates, places and notes; red ink for the route and the punchlines; Space Mono (fine) for coordinates, km and altitude.
- **Motion:**
  - A page turn at each new day.
  - The red route draws itself on the 8ths.
  - Blooms land on downbeats, and pencil lines draw in over a beat.
  - Slow pushes on the landscapes, counters for km and altitude, one book turn, one riffle.
