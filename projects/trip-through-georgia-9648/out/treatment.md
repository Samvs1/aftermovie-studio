## Concept: "Yes, no offroad"
A hand-kept road-trip journal. Taped prints on kraft and paper pages, handwritten place and date notes, and a road atlas that keeps drawing the route. The song says *cave*; this trip found three: Uplistsikhe, Prometheus and Vardzia. Verse 3 goes literally underground. The jokes come from your brief and run through the film:
- **The rental rule.** A typed contract note reads "Rule 1: NO OFFROAD", you answer "Yes, no offroad.", and the punchline is the tyre stuck in a pothole in Gori: "the main road." Called back at Tskaltubo's NO ENTRY tape: "Yes, no entry."
- **Two words of Georgian.** A "Gamarjoba!" speech bubble on arrival. At the Kutaisi market, on "I will not hear what you have to say", a vendor's bubble says ბლა ბლა ბლა (Georgian letters for "bla bla bla") and yours answers "Madloba!". The ending is "Madloba!", then your line.
- **Khinkali every day.** It starts in the verse 1 food run ("khinkali (again)") and comes back on the stats card: "khinkali: every day".
- **Your own labels, verbatim:** "Stalin top G ⚒️", "Crazy city", "Big chill", "Thx Keti for the tour!", "The border" (with a drawn RU flag; Windows can't draw flag emoji).

**Signature devices (new for this film):**
- The **route map with a little rental car** driving it on the beat: once at the interlude (Tbilisi → Kazbegi) and once at the finale (the whole loop).
- **Speech bubbles** in Georgian and English.
- A typed **rental-contract** note.
- One **print that turns upside down** on the lyric.
- Three short **video inserts**: Tom's waterfall scream, the priests singing, Tom and the stray dog.

No VHS, no polaroids, no film leader: those belong to Time to Pretend and Cool Kids.
